  (v''')(a) Is the identity I derived -- for the block matrix Gr = [[Lam,K],[K^T,I_q]], its minimal
      eigenpair (x;y), lambda = lambda_min(Gr) < lambda_min(Lam) implies
      K^T (Lam - lambda I)^{-1} K y = (1 - lambda) y,

      as an eigenvalue certificate?

HITS THAT TOUCH THE QUESTION
  (1) arXiv:2510.02900 (2025-10-03) V. Janssens, K. Meerbergen, W. Michiels,
      "Linearizing a nonlinear eigenvalue problem with quadratic rational eigenvector nonlinearities".
      => Directly in the neighbourhood of (v''')(a): a problem of the form
         [Lam - lambda I, K; K^T, ...] with a rational dependence on lambda is exactly what the NEP
         literature calls a *rational eigenvalue problem* Q(lambda) z = 0, and "linearization" is the
         standard device. If this paper (or the quadratic-eigenvector-nonlinearities family) already
         contains the step "the q-block of an eigenvector satisfies the rational equation obtained by
         eliminating x", then prop:tight is a restatement and must be demoted to a remark.
         NOT YET READ -- abstract-level only. Needs per-entry verification (absget.sh).
  (2) arXiv:1907.10972 F. M. Dopico, S. Marcaida, M. C. Quintana, P. Van Dooren,
      "Local Linearizations of Rational Matrices with Application to Rational Approximations of
      Nonlinear Eigenvalue Problems".
      => Same verdict path as (1): the rational-matrix-linearisation machinery is mature and mine is a
         2x2 case. Cite-or-defer decision belongs to A's novelty pass.
  (3) arXiv:1903.11395 S. Pozza, M. S. Pranic, "The Gauss quadrature for general linear functionals,
      Lanczos algorithm, and minimal partial realization".
      => Touches (v"")(b)/(v''')(b) from the *computational* side: Gauss quadrature of a linear
         functional = tridiagonal (Jacobi/Lanczos) matrix, which is how prop:gauss computes its nodes.
         This is why I expect (v"")(b) to come back "known": the moment hierarchy I run is one Lanczos
         step away from a standard statement.
  (4) arXiv:2211.04729 / 2607.14511 P. Kabaila, "Custom-made Gauss quadrature for statisticians" (and
      the adaptive-precision sequel). Not about bounds from below; noted only to show that the
      quadrature-for-matrix-functionals route is active and that my [B']-style moment check is not a
      substitute for reading these.
  (5) arXiv:1311.0326 Cacciapuoti-Maltsev-Schlein "Bounds for the Stieltjes Transform and the Density
      of States of Wigner Matrices"; arXiv:2609.15862 Najnudel-Nikeghbali "stationary Stieltjes
      transforms". Both bound Stieltjes transforms of *random* matrices by different machinery
      (resolvent estimates, not Gauss rules) -- useful as a contrast sentence in sec:position, no more.
